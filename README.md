# redwater
REDWATER — A Frontier Tale

You ride into Redwater with a rusted six and no side. By the end of it, the whole county knows which one you picked.

A top-down open-world western RPG that runs in a single HTML file — no engine, no downloads, no image or audio assets. Every grain of sand, every gunshot and every building is drawn or synthesised at runtime.

The county. Six thousand acres of bleached flats, red mesa and dry riverbed, drawn like a spaghetti western printed on an 1880s wanted poster: flat lithographic shapes, heavy blacks, one low raking sun that swings through a seven-minute day. Twelve named places — the town of Redwater, the outlaw hold at Black Mesa, Copper Creek Mine, Hargrave Ranch, Perdition Depot, Boot Hill, the Old Mission — and eight buildings you walk into, not menus that open: the Dry Gulch saloon with a piano man who only knows one tune, the sheriff's office with a prisoner who swears he was nowhere near that horse.

No side, then a side. You start as a drifter nobody's decided about. Sheriff Abel Cordell offers tin. Silas Kane offers the other thing. Both branches have their own chain of work — bounties, a stagecoach to hold, a raid on the mesa, or a bank to crack and a sheriff to hang — and both converge on Augustus Hargrave, the baron who's been paying both sides of every argument in the county. When the story runs out, the bounty board and the job board keep printing paper forever.

Twenty guns, and four that wake up. Common iron is pale and worn and does nothing but fire. Fine steel is blued and brass-banded. Rare arms carry an ability on Q — fan the hammer, dragon's breath, a round that ignores wood, stone and men. The four legendaries carry that and an awakening on F: a cinematic that charges as you deal damage.

Dead Man's Hand — time stops, every soul in sight is marked, and you finish the hand. Two cards ride the air beside it.
Judgment — four expanding rings of fire, and the ground remembers. Its twin mouths burn even at rest.
Widowmaker — the world drains to ink and bone and one shot crosses all of it.
Iron Horse — six barrels, no conscience, and ghost horses through the smoke.

Also in there: Dead Eye slow-motion aiming, dive-rolls, horses you can buy or break in, dynamite, a wanted level that sends posses after you, quick-draw duels on a full cinematic screen, ten perks, and a save that keeps your ride.

Controls: WASD move · mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB Dead Eye · Space roll · E use · R reload · Q ability · F awakening · G dynamite · Tab journal · M map
